Privacy Policy for Fabled

Effective date: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Loose Cannon Games LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use the mobile game Fabled (the "Game"). By using the Game, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated automatically as you play.

Account information

  • Email address (via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In)
  • A unique user identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication
  • Optional display name and profile image you choose in-game

Gameplay information

  • Your in-game progress: account level, currencies, owned characters, inventory, achievements, match history, leaderboard standings
  • Choices you make in-game including selected cosmetics, equipped items, and team configurations
  • Server-side records of in-app purchases (if any)

AI-generated content

  • Text prompts assembled from your in-game choices and sent to AI image-generation and prompt-rewriting providers
  • Images those providers return, stored in our hosting bucket so they persist across devices

Diagnostic information

  • Crash reports and basic performance telemetry via Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Device model, operating system version, and Game build version, used for debugging
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage analytics

Bug reports you submit

  • Any text, screenshots, prompts, or device information you choose to attach when you submit an in-Game bug report. These are delivered to our private support channel via a webhook.

We do not collect: contacts, calendar, microphone audio, camera images (other than what you explicitly attach to a bug report), precise location, SMS messages, or your phone's photo library.

2. How we use your information

  • Provide and operate the Game (save progress, run matches, deliver rewards)
  • Generate AI-driven cosmetic art at your request
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and cheating
  • Communicate critical service updates if needed

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling for marketing, or sale to third parties.

4. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from within the Game's settings (Profile → Delete Account). Deletion is permanent and removes:

  • Your profile and gameplay records
  • Generated images stored under your account
  • Your authentication record

Crash reports and aggregated analytics may persist in anonymized form after account deletion, as they are not tied to your personal identity.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, contact us at the email below and we will delete the account.

6. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted transit (HTTPS), Firebase security rules to restrict access, and per-user data isolation. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee against unauthorized access.

7. International data transfers

Our service providers may process your data in countries other than your country of residence (notably the United States). By using the Game you consent to this transfer.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (EEA, UK, California, and others), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, email us at the address below.
