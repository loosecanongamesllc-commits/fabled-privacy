Delete Your Fabled Account & Data

Fabled is developed by Loose Cannon Games LLC. This page explains how to request deletion of your Fabled account and all associated data.

How to request deletion

Email loosecanongamesllc@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Fabled account, with the subject line “Delete My Account.” Please include your in-game player name so we can locate your account.

We will verify ownership and complete the deletion within 30 days of the request, and email you to confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account login and authentication record (email address and credentials)
  • All in-game data: your Fables, currencies (gold, diamonds, shards), items and inventory, account and Fable levels, PvP rank, and achievements
  • All AI-generated Fable artwork stored on our servers

What is kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted database backups are automatically purged within 7 days.
  • Anonymized crash and diagnostic logs (used only to fix bugs) may persist for up to 90 days before being automatically deleted. These do not identify you after your account is removed.

After these retention periods elapse, no personally identifying data about you is retained.
